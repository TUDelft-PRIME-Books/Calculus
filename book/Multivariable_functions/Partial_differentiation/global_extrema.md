# Global extrema


In this section we cover:

- Global minimum and maximum
- Closed and bounded domains (Refer to closed intervals in section 1.2, also mention the term _compact_)
- Extreme values on closed and bounded domains in $\mathbb{R}^2$
- Extreme values on closed and bounded domains in $\mathbb{R}^n$

Remark: A critical point of the function on the boundary of a compact domain is considered a valid candidate, as the boundary is part of the domain.

::::{prf:theorem} 
:label: Thm:GlobExtrnd:EVT
Let $D$ be a closed and bounded subset of $\mathbb{R}^2$ and let $f:D\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ be a continuous function. Then $f$ attains a global maximum and a global minimum on $D$.
